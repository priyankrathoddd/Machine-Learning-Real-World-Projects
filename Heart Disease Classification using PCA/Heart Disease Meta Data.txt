Dataset Description
This dataset comprises 76 attributes, but most published studies use a subset of 14. Among these, the Cleveland database is the most utilized by ML researchers. The target field (num) indicates the presence of heart disease, with values ranging from 0 (no disease) to 4 (presence of disease). Research focuses on distinguishing between the presence (values 1, 2, 3, 4) and absence (value 0) of heart disease. Patient names and social security numbers have been anonymized.
